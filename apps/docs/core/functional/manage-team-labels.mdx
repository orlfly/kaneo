---
title: Manage Team Labels
description: Create, edit, and delete reusable labels at the team level
---

Labels help your team categorize tasks consistently across all projects. Team-level labels are defined once and available everywhere: update the name or color, and it changes everywhere that label is used.

## Open team labels

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Admin -> Teams**.
3. Pick the team whose labels you want to manage.
4. Open **Labels** for that team.

You'll see a list of all existing team labels with their colors and names.

## Create a label

1. Click **Create Label**.
2. Enter a name (e.g. `bug`, `feature`, `api`).
3. Pick a color from the 9 available options:
   - Stone, Slate, Lavender, Sage, Forest, Amber, Terracotta, Rose, Crimson
4. Click **Create Label**.

The label is now available for assignment to tasks across all projects in the team.

## Edit a label

1. Click the pencil icon next to the label you want to change.
2. Update the name, color, or both.
3. Click **Save**.

Changes cascade to every task currently using that label, with no manual re-assignment needed.

## Delete a label

1. Click the trash icon next to the label.
2. Confirm deletion in the dialog.

Deleting a team label removes it from **all** tasks that use it. This cannot be undone.

## Permissions

Managing team labels requires an **owner** role in the team. If you don't see the edit or delete controls, ask a team owner to update your role.

## Best practices

- Use consistent naming: `bug`, `feature`, `chore` for work type; `api`, `web`, `infra` for domain
- Assign distinct colors to different categories so they're easy to scan on the board
- Keep the total number of labels manageable, since too many labels dilute their usefulness
